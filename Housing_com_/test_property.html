Located in the Ahmedabad district of Gujarat, Satellite is a great locality when it comes to residential properties. It has a population of approx 66800 with approx 35000 male domination population & 31000 female dominating population. The Satellite covers an area of about 2.5 square kilometers. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport is 10.73 km away, and it is the nearest airport from Satellite. The nearest railway station from Satellite is Vastrapur which is about 1.71 km from the city center. It is a well-established & developed residential area where you can easily live a lavish life. It is situated in the western suburbs of Ahmedabad. Sarkhej Gandhinagar Highway and 132 Feet Ring Road are the two main significant roads that connect Satellite to various urban parts of Ahmedabad. This place provides decent availability to top-ranked educational institutes in the area like New Samarth English School, Ahmedabad International School, Shri Narayana Central School, Satellite School for Children, and MLC College. This place is the perfect area to live a healthy & well-established life because it has educational institutes, metro station, airport, railway station & many corporate parks as well. Read More
